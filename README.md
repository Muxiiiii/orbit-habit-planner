# orbit-habit-planner
Give every habit the hours it deserves.
Orbit is a habit planner built around time. Tell it what you want to do and how many hours it deserves each day, week, month or year. Orbit splits that time across your calendar, times every session, and celebrates when you hit the mark.

Link to the web version: https://muxiiiii.github.io/orbit-habit-planner/

## Install it like an app
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** open the link, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap ⋮, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **Computer (Chrome / Edge):** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

## Your data
Your habits are saved only in your own browser. Use **Settings → Backup** to download a backup file, and **Restore from backup** to move your habits to another browser or device.
