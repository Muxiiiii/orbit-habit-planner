About Orbit

Give every habit the hours it deserves.

Orbit is a habit planner built around time. Tell it what you want to do and how many hours it deserves each day, week, month or year. Orbit splits that time across your calendar, times every session, and celebrates when you hit the mark.

01 · The idea

Plan in hours, not checkboxes.

A checkbox says you showed up. Hours say you went deep. Orbit tracks the time you give each habit so progress stays honest.

05 · Track

Press start. Orbit tells you when you’re there.

Tap Start or a time slot to begin. When you reach today’s goal you get the celebration you picked: a rocket launch, a moonrise, a sunrise, scattering stars or fireworks, with a sound of your choice.

06 · Carry over

Extra time is never wasted.

Keep counting past your goal and the extra minutes go to your Extra time bank. Orbit suggests the next days to lighten, and nothing changes until you confirm. You can also choose the days yourself or keep the time as a bonus.

How to use Orbit

Step 1

Add a habit

Press “New habit”. Name it, choose daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, and enter the total time you want for that period.

Step 2

Check the split

Orbit fills in a plan. Change any day’s minutes, mark rest days, or set time slots for daily habits. Days you leave alone share what’s left.

Step 3

Start the timer

Press Start on a habit or tap one of its time slots. The timer keeps running if you switch tabs or reload the page.

Step 4

Celebrate and adjust

Choose a celebration and sound, or turn it off. Pick whether the timer stops at the goal or keeps counting. Extra minutes wait in the Extra time bank until you confirm which days they lighten.
